About

Engineering AI that solves real problems

I'm Aliha Imran, an AI/ML Engineer and Generative AI Developer focused on turning large language models into working systems, not demos. My work sits at the intersection of machine learning, retrieval-augmented generation, and backend engineering — I build the pipelines that let AI reason over real data and act on it reliably.

Day to day, that means building RAG pipelines with FastAPI and vector databases, designing agentic AI workflows that chain reasoning and tool use, and writing the Python architecture that keeps it all fast and maintainable. I care as much about system design and clean APIs as I do about model output.
